# Autarkia

The world lives... Without you.

> From greek _αὐτάρκεια_ (autarkeia) - "self-sufficient"

Fully autonomous NPCs that survive just like a player. They drop into the world just like a normal player. Empty inventory, only with their desire to survive.

## Features

- Social, curious, self-sufficient. They can hunt, gather, farm, ranch, build, mine, explore, plan for the future.  
- They develop relationships, they protect themselves and help eachother, they notice and react to the world.  
- You can trade with them, you can help them, protect them. You can join their settlement, or invite them to your house. Form friendships, get closer to them, maybe fall in love?  
- But you can also try to steal from them, destroy their buildings, or attack them. They will defend themselves, or flee in fear. But they will remember your actions. They will hold grudges, spread the word, and maybe even seek revenge.

## A new person, a new world

A small story. The idea is to make all of this completely possible with the mod.

- **Day 1**  
I spawned in a new world, no idea where I am. I'll punch wood, but a tool would come in handy...  
I'll need food soon, those berries look good, but there are some pigs just over there.  
This area looks safe. I'll place a crafting table, craft some basic tools.

- **Day 2**  
Huh, there's someone else there, they have a pickaxe. I wonder if they want to work together. It's lonely around here.  
**"Hey! You!"**  
She was friendly, we teamed up. _Charlotte_ was her name. She didn't have a shelter yet, but she did find some food. We can build a lot faster together.  

- **Day 3**  
_Charlotte_ went mining, I'll clear a better area for our house. That cave isn't a good long term base.
She brought some cobblestone and coal. That's great, we can make torches and smelt some food. I prepared a ranch for the pigs I found earlier, soon we'll be able to kill them for a steady food supply.  

- **Day 4**  
Two other people showed up. They were friendly, asked if we wanted to trade. They had a lot of bread, and wanted some of our iron ore. We weren't ready to use that iron yet, we have to evolve more before we can use it. They left happy, after telling their settlement was not far from here. They said we would be welcome in there!

- **Day 5**  
Someone else different showed up. They looked just like us, but a bit different. They seemed curious, came closer to us.  
**"Hi, who are you?"**  
**"I'm `Player`!"**  
Hmm, that's a weird name. But they do look like us, and they seem to know how to survive. I asked them to join us, and they did. I showed them around, and introduced them to _Charlotte_.  

- **Day 9**  
That `Player` one sure is smart! They built a farm and a lot of chests. They also built a house for themselves and expanded the ranch. They knew how to use the iron we found, and made some great tools for us.  
They found some other people lost around the trees and asked to join us. We are evolving fast! There's 5 people already, all working together, I focused on ranching, while _Charlotte_ focused on [cooking](https://modrinth.com/project/autarkia-delight).  
`Player` is a great builder, they planned a great house and a tavern for us. _David_ said he'd clear the area tomorrow, and _Doris_ would gather the needed wood.

- **Day 12**  
Yesterday... We lost _Doris_.  She was out on the forest, we heard her scream.  
Me and _David_ ran to her, too late. We only found her axe.  
She was attacked by a zombie, we killed it easily, but she couldn't defend herself alone. We need to be more careful...  
`Player` planned a wall around our settlement. We have enough people to designate guards, and set up a watchtower near the forest.

- **Day 18**  
Me and __Charlotte__ are going to walk around together.  
We want to explore the area, look for some new resources. We want more types of wood, building everything from spruce is starting to look boring. We can afford to build prettier stuff.  
I grabbed some food, weared my armor, and am bringing my sword and shield. _Charlotte_ is bringing her bow and arrows that `Player` made for her. They protect us so well, we lost no one since that wall was built. 

- **Day 32**  
A lot happened since my last diary log. Me and _Charlotte_ started dating on that walk, several days ago. We moved in together, a house just for us. We're very happy together.  
Our small settlement became a village, and it's still growing. At least 20 people already. The tavern brings a lot of visitors, bringing [exotic](https://modrinth.com/project/autarkia-compat) goods. It also attracts bandits, we had a few incidents recently but our guards did a great job defending us.  
Tomorrow, we'll have a festival. All the people will gather and celebrate together. We will have music, food, and [wine](https://modrinth.com/project/lets-do-autarkia), that `Player` made. I hope that everyone has a great time.

- **Day 50**  
We just survived a raid. A group of dozen bandits attacked our village at dusk. They were well equipped, wearing iron armor and swords. They were a lot stronger than the usual bandits we encounter.  
Some guards died, but we defended ourselves. _Edward_ was a great fighter, and a great friend. I'll remember him. His fiancée _Eleanor_ was devastated, I didn't see her in 2 days. I hope she recovers.  
Other than that, we are doing good. We're repairing the walls and replanting the burnt crops, but overall, just fine. `Player` is a great leader, they planned most of the defense, if it wasn't for them, we definitely would've lost a lot more people.  
Me and _Charlotte_ are going to bring them a gift tomorrow, to thank them for all their help. We're very grateful for everything they did for us.
Speaking of her, she is pregnant! We are very happy, and can't wait to meet our child. We will name him _Arthur_.

## Versions and compatibility

**Fabric**, for Minecraft **1.21.11**, **26.1.x** and **26.2.x**. Needs **Fabric API**.

First-party support for **Forge / NeoForge** through [Sinytra Connector](https://connector.sinytra.org/). All features in this mod are 100% compatible with it. I don't want to lock this mod to a single loader, but also don't have to build 2 compat layers. May change my mind in the future and actually build a Forge/NeoForge compat layer.

For now, this isn't expanded to 1.20.1 and 1.21.1, because the Person entity is (currently) limited by being dependent on the 1.21.9+ Avatar entity and other limitations. This might change in the future.
